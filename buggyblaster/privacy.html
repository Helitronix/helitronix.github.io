Buggy Blaster Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Buggy Blaster does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account to make, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your high scores, initials and settings are saved on your own device. They are not sent anywhere.

Playing with your iPhone as a controller

If you choose to use your iPhone as the controller for Buggy Blaster on a Mac or another computer, the game asks for permission to use your local network. Your iPhone then sends its tilt and button presses directly to that computer over your own Wi-Fi. Nothing goes over the internet.

Game controllers

Game controllers are read by the game on your device only.

Beta testing

If you test an early version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with us what TestFlight collects: the name and email address you were invited with, basic information about your device, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use these only to improve the game, and we don't share them with anyone.

Children

Buggy Blaster is for players of all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
