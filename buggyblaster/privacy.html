Buggy Blaster Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Buggy Blaster does not collect any personal information. There is no account to make, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your settings and your local high-score table are saved on your own device.

World high scores (Game Center)

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center and the high-score table is set to Global, then when you choose Submit at the end of a game, your score and the three initials you entered are sent to Game Center. They appear in the world high-score table for that difficulty, and other Game Center players can see them with your Game Center name. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy. To keep your scores on your device, choose Cancel instead, or set the high-score table to Local in Settings.

With the table set to Global, the game also sends Game Center a running count of how many games you have played and for how long. Everyone's counts are added up for the "Buggy Blasters Worldwide" page, and Game Center players can see each count with its Game Center name. With the table set to Local, the counts stay on your device.

World high scores on Android (Google Play Games)

On Android, Google Play Games does the same job as Game Center. If you are signed in to Play Games and the high-score table is set to Global, your score and initials are sent to Play Games when you choose Submit, along with your running counts of games and time played. Other Play Games players can see them with your Play Games name. Google runs Play Games under its own privacy policy. Choose Cancel, or set the table to Local, to keep everything on your device.

Deleting your data

Everything the game keeps on your device (settings, local high scores) is removed when you delete the app.

Scores, initials and play counts sent to Google Play Games are kept by Google until you delete them: open your Play Games profile (or the Play Games settings on your phone), find Buggy Blaster under your games, and delete its data. Deleting your Play Games profile removes it for every game. Scores sent to Apple's Game Center are managed in the Game Center settings on your iPhone. If you need help, write to the address below and we'll walk you through it; we keep no copy of this data ourselves.

Playing with your iPhone as a controller

If you choose to use your iPhone as the controller for Buggy Blaster on a Mac or another computer, the game asks for permission to use your local network. Your iPhone then sends its tilt and button presses directly to that computer over your own Wi-Fi. Nothing goes over the internet.

Game controllers

Game controllers are read by the game on your device only.

Beta testing

If you test an early version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with us what TestFlight collects: the name and email address you were invited with, basic information about your device, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use these only to improve the game, and we don't share them with anyone.

If you test an early version through Google Play, Google shares with us what Play Console collects: basic information about your device, crash reports, and any feedback you choose to send. We use these the same way.

Children

Buggy Blaster is for players of all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
