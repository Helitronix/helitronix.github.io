Buggy Blaster

An arcade game by Joel Rosenzweig

Drive your buggy across the land, jump the craters and boulders, and blast the mines and UFOs before they get you. Eleven levels, each with its own scenery, and three difficulties.

Buggy Blaster first came out for the iPhone in 2009. A new version for iPhone, Android and Mac is being tested now, with sharper art, wide-screen side panels and world high-score tables.
